## Free Plugins & Tools (Community Edition)
We believe in supporting the scholarly community. We offer a suite of free, open-source plugins designed to help smaller journals and startup presses professionalize their workflow.

| Plugin | Purpose & Impact |
| :--- | :--- |
| **[ReachMetric Lite](https://github.com/OJSpro/reachmetric-lite)** | **Analytics Transparency:** Adds clean, real-time "Abstract View" and "PDF Download" counters to article and issue pages. |
| **[Backlink Tracker](https://github.com/OJSpro/backlink-tracker)** | **Beyond Citations:** Integrates with Moz/Ahrefs/Semrush to show real-world web popularity and scholarly as well as non-scholarly references to your research like references from blogs, news, whitepapers, etc. |
| **[Related Resources](https://github.com/OJSpro/related-resources)** | **Reader Engagement:** Allows editors to curate external links and supplementary materials, turning article pages into comprehensive research hubs. |
| **[SiteMode](https://github.com/OJSpro/site-mode)** | **Maintenance Control:** A simple utility to toggle 'Maintenance Mode' or 'Coming Soon' banners with countdown timers for OJS/OMP 3.4. |
| **[Issue Article Sort](https://github.com/OJSpro/issue-toc-sort)** | **UX Precision:** Gives journal managers granular, automated control over how articles are ordered and displayed within an issue. |

*...and many more in active development.*
